Milestone 3 NEON tile-size sweep
================================

Method
Ran make run TILE=<size> once each for tiles 32, 64, 128, and 256,
in that order. The benchmark uses the same seeded inputs for every
tile size. It checks the NEON and Accelerate outputs against the
sequential result, then averages five CPU-time measurements per
implementation after one warm-up call.

Times are in milliseconds. A negative change means tile 32 took
less CPU time than tile 64, the current default.
